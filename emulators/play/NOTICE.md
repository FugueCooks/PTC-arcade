# Experimental Play! PS2 browser runtime

The files `main.js`, `main.css`, and `Play.wasm` are an unmodified snapshot of the official experimental Play!.js build published at <https://playjs.purei.org/> on 2026-08-22.

Upstream source: <https://github.com/jpd002/Play->  
Upstream project: <https://purei.org/>  
License: `LICENSE.txt`

This runtime is experimental. It accepts ISO, CSO, CHD, ISZ, BIN, and ELF files, requires no external BIOS, and is intentionally isolated from the stable EmulatorJS PS1/N64 player.
